The Story

From Marketing Student to AI-Powered Agency Founder

After completing my BBA in Marketing at Independent University Bangladesh, I spent years studying how the world's best brands acquire customers — and noticed a gap: most small and medium businesses couldn't afford the agencies that truly understood digital advertising.

I started Signal Digital to close that gap. By building every workflow around AI tools and automation, I'm able to deliver results that rival large agencies — at a fraction of the cost, with far more personalized attention.

"Most agencies sell you a team. I sell you outcomes. Every campaign I run is built around one question: what will actually move the needle for this specific business?"

Today, Signal Digital serves clients across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia — helping e-commerce brands, SaaS startups, coaches, clinics, and local service businesses acquire customers and scale revenue through Meta Ads, Social Media Management, and custom AI tools.

My long-term mission is to build the most efficient, transparent, and results-driven marketing agency operating out of South Asia — proving that geography is no barrier to world-class work.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't wall posters. They're the actual principles that drive every campaign, every report, and every client relationship.

Radical Transparency

Every report shows the full picture — wins, misses, and what we're doing about it. No vanity metrics, no spin. You'll always know exactly where your money is going and what it's producing.

Results Over Activity

We don't measure success by how many posts we published or how many ads we ran. We measure it by your bottom line — new customers acquired, revenue generated, cost per acquisition reduced.

AI-Augmented Intelligence

Every strategy we build is powered by AI tools — for faster research, sharper audience targeting, better creative insights, and smarter optimization. Technology makes us faster. Experience makes us better.

Direct Partnership

You work with Shakib directly — not a junior account manager who rotates every few months. That means consistent communication, real ownership, and a partner who actually cares about your growth.

Honest Communication

If something isn't working, we'll tell you before you ask. If a strategy needs to change, we'll say so clearly and explain why. We'd rather have a difficult conversation than let a campaign underperform in silence.

Continuous Learning

Meta's algorithm changes. Audiences shift. What worked last quarter might not work this quarter. We stay ahead by continuously testing, learning, and adapting — so your campaigns always reflect what's working now.

Our Journey

How Signal Digital Was Built

2022

The Foundation

Graduated with BBA in Marketing from Independent University Bangladesh. Began freelancing in digital marketing — running Meta Ads for local Bangladeshi businesses while learning the fundamentals of paid advertising and content strategy.

2023

Going International

Launched on Fiverr and Upwork, targeting US, UK, and Australian clients in e-commerce and coaching niches. First international clients validated the model — high quality work is geography-agnostic. Began building AI-powered marketing workflows to increase efficiency.

2024

Signal Digital is Born

Officially launched Signal Digital as a branded agency. Expanded service offerings to include Social Media Management and custom AI-powered lead magnet tools. Reached 30+ clients across 4 markets. Built the Revenue ROI Calculator as a portfolio proof piece — live at signal-roi-calculator.netlify.app.

2025–2026

Scaling with Purpose

Grew to 50+ clients across US, UK, EU, and AU. Deepened AI integration across all service lines. Launched this professional agency website. Long-term goal: establish Signal Digital as the most trusted AI-powered marketing agency built from South Asia, serving global markets.
