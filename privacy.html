Privacy Policy

Signal Digital ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services. Please read this carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out our contact form, request a strategy call, or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Full name and email address
  • Phone number or WhatsApp number (if provided)
  • Business name and industry
  • Marketing budget and service interests
  • Messages and communications you send us

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information including:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page
  • Referring URL (how you found our website)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

Information from Third-Party Services

We use EmailJS to process form submissions. When you submit a contact form, your data passes through EmailJS's servers. Please review EmailJS's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries — We use your contact details to reply to your messages and schedule strategy calls
  • To provide our services — Client information is used to deliver and manage the marketing services you've engaged us for
  • To improve our website — Anonymised usage data helps us understand how to make our website more useful
  • To send service-related communications — We may send you emails related to your enquiry or active engagement with us
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Ever.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — When you submit a contact form, you consent to us using your data to respond
  • Legitimate Interests — We have a legitimate interest in processing enquiries and improving our services
  • Contract — When you become a client, processing is necessary to fulfil our contractual obligations

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Enquiries that don't convert to clients — Deleted within 12 months
  • Active client data — Retained for the duration of the engagement plus 2 years
  • Financial records — Retained for 7 years as required by applicable law

5. Third-Party Services

Our website and services interact with the following third-party services:

  • EmailJS — Form submission processing (Privacy Policy)
  • Netlify — Website hosting (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts — Font delivery (no cookies set)
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — For clients' advertising campaigns only, under separate data processing agreements

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. Google Fonts may set a cookie to deliver fonts efficiently. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use our website.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing — Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Right to object — Object to our processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at rezwanahmed1050@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. Our website is served over HTTPS. Form data is transmitted via EmailJS's encrypted servers. We do not store payment card details — all payments are processed by third-party providers (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Payoneer).

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Signal Digital is based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. When you submit data to us, it may be processed in Bangladesh and/or through services located in the United States (EmailJS, Netlify). We take steps to ensure your data receives appropriate protection in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:
