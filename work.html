The Challenge

A home renovation company in Texas spending $800/month on Google Ads with declining returns. Average cost-per-lead had climbed to $140 with low-quality inquiries from tire-kickers. Needed a better system.

What We Did

Moved budget to Meta Ads with a before/after creative strategy showing completed renovation projects. Geo-targeted ZIP codes within 30 miles of the business.
